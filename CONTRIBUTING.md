# Contributing

Thank you for helping improve these Computer Science learning resources.

- Submit original explanations and questions, or content you are authorized to share.
- Keep explanations accurate, clear, and useful to learners.
- Include the topic, intended level, correct answer, and a short explanation for practice questions.
- Cite sources for factual claims and respect licenses and attribution requirements.
- Do not submit personal information, credentials, confidential material, or copied question banks.
- For corrections or questions, contact [contact@examrig.com](mailto:contact@examrig.com).
